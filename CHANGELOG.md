## Unreleased

- **2026-10-22 — the regulated stock register: one app for any regulated stock, each market's books a data set.** New sample **S41** (`samples/s41_regulated_register/`, `pp_rsr`). A data set declares a market's books (controlled medicine, liquor, tobacco, duty-free): which items each takes, up to three routing conditions in the save-rule form, its columns and their sources, its numbering, balance, correction mode and retention; one template prints every book. The first data set is Kenya's three pharmacy books (Poisons Book Form 16, the Prescription Book, the NPP ledger Annex II), each marked *a starting point, not certified for the country*; a test proves a second data set needs no code change. What installs today: the register's own columns on the Desk sale and delivery and as the till's sale details, the approver role, a report and a workspace. ⚠ The books themselves wait for the register door in the POS app: their declarations and prints are built (`door_declaration.json`) and tested against fixture lines, and install in the app's next version, once the door is there.

- **2026-10-22 — S40, expiry and recall.** A new sample, [S40](samples/s40_expiry_recall/README.md), and its recipe, [Expiry and recall](recipes/expiry_recall.md): a recall notice whose *Block these batches* button switches the lots off at every till at once and traces each store's stock and the customers already supplied from the stock ledger; a disposal record that locks once the regulator's visit is recorded, and a document check on Stock Entry that refuses a controlled disposal without it; a document check on Purchase Receipt that refuses short-dated lots unless the line carries a Letter of Undertaking with its days, and flags the ERP's own receipts instead; an expiry worklist per store. A new need card, *A recall by batch, traced to stores and customers, and disposal on the record*.

- **2026-10-22 — pharmacy counter rules: a check at Pay and document checks on the Desk, with refills reserved in the shop's own records.** New sample **S39** (`samples/s39_pharmacy_counter/`) and recipe [Pharmacy counter rules](recipes/pharmacy_counter.md); the need cards *A prescription number before payment* and *Prescriptions kept as records* now point at it. Drug schedules, pharmacists and prescriptions are records the shop fills, nothing written in per country. A prescription line is paid only against this customer's prescription, in date for its class, with a fill and quantity left; a pharmacist-only line only by a pharmacist whose licence is in date; a controlled line needs the customer's identity fields; caps per sale and per patient hold; dispensed medicine is refused at a return and on a Desk credit note. Each fill is a record the service makes under the patient's next number, so two tills cannot spend the last one; it is released when the shop says so (a sale never booked by its day's close, a cancelled sale or document), never on a timer. Two till services (the dispensary refuses when down, the front lets the sale through) and three Desk services (Sales Order, Delivery Note, Sales Invoice), spending one fill per document chain.
