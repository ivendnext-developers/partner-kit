# Claim a need — tell us you will build something on our *next* list, so we hold back from it

**For:** an iVendNext Partner who will build, and sell, something our roadmap lists as *next* ([What iVendNext builds](../site/pages/roadmap.md)). These forms are for onboarded members ([who can file](README.md)); you do not need your company's first word yet. **Reference:** the roadmap's *Claim a need* section, and *Build your business on iVendNext's apps* ([`../site/pages/business.md`](../site/pages/business.md)).

A claim is how you keep us out of your way: once we accept it, we do not start that need for 90 days, and the roadmap shows it as *Claimed*, with no name and no date. **Every member reads every issue,** so other members can see your claim, as they see every form.

## When to use it

- A need on the roadmap's *next* list is one you will build: an app for the App library, or a solution you will deliver to customers in your market.

**Use another form, or none, if:** the need is *being built* or *listed* already (it has started, or it is answered; where it is one of our free kit apps, copy it and make it your customer's own version); the need is on the *you build* list (it is yours already, no claim needed); you need a door that does not exist to build it → [extension-point request](point_request.md).

## The form

```markdown
# Claim a need: <the need, as the roadmap's next list names it>

Company: <your company's name, as you registered it with the iVendNext Partner programme>

## 1. What you will build
<The app or the solution, who uses it, and how a retailer will get it: listed in the App library for every retailer, or delivered customer by customer.>

## 2. Your market
<The countries or the region you will sell it in.>

## 3. Where you are with it (optional)
<A design, a first version on your sandbox, a customer waiting for it.>
```

## What we may ask you next, in the issue

How far along you are, if you left section 3 empty · the doors your build uses · whether you will list it in the App library.

## What happens after you send it

- It is given an **id**, which comes back to you in the issue.
- It is answered in the issue with one of: **accepted** (the roadmap shows the need as *Claimed*), **needs more** or **declined** (with the reason, for example that the need is being built already).
- **An accepted claim holds 90 days from the day you filed it.** Within them we do not start that need.
- When your app is listed in the App library, *partner first* applies for as long as it answers the need: we build no free app against it, and we feature yours. A claim that ends without a listed app ends there: the need is no longer shown as *Claimed*, and we may start it.
