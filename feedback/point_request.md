# Extension-point request — ask for a new extension point, or for more from a published one

**For:** an iVendNext Partner, a licensed customer's IT team or the CitiXsys Consulting Team whose blueprint has a need that no rung of the ladder can meet. These forms are for onboarded members ([who can file](README.md)). **Reference:** the extension-point catalogue (guide chapter 9, [The extension points, and how to ask for a new one](../guide/09_points_and_requests.md)).

An **extension point** is a fixed question we ask your service at a published moment, with a closed set of answers and a stated behaviour when your service is down or the till is offline. It is the last rung of the ladder. Describe the **need**, not the mechanism: a request that needs money changed in a hook, an override or in-tenant code is read as a request for the need to be met another way.

## When to use it

- Your blueprint has a row with no door, or whose door is marked *needs a point request*, **after you tried to combine the doors you have** (rung 6 of the ladder); the issue names the compositions you tried and why each falls short.
- A published extension point lacks something you need: a field in the data in, an answer, a moment in the sale. Say which one and what is missing.

**Use another form if:** the page that explains a door is wrong → [recipe or document gap](recipe_or_document_gap.md); a kit piece misbehaves → [kit defect](kit_defect.md); you only want to see a pattern worked → [sample request](sample_request.md).

## The form

```markdown
# Extension-point request: <one-line title>

## 1. What you need to do
<The need in the customer's business terms, and the step it concerns: sale, return, scan, customer add or back office. No names, no shoppers' data.>

## 2. The closest extension point
<Its name as guide chapter 9 gives it and what is missing, or "none".>

## 3. If it is never built
<What you do instead, and what it costs the customer.>

- [ ] It touches a sale, a price or a payment
```

## What we may ask you next, in the issue

Your blueprint and the rows this concerns · the doors you tried and why each falls short · the facts your service needs (the data in, with any personal data marked) · the closed list of answers you need · what the till should do when your service is down · the longest the cashier should wait · what should happen on a till with no network · one sample request and answer, including a refusal · the busiest hour's volume (calls, stores, tills).

A request that does not say what happens when your service is *down*, *slow* or *offline* gets those three questions back.

## What happens after you send it

- It is given an **id**, which comes back to you in the issue.
- It is answered in the issue with one of: **answered** (a published door already meets it, and the answer says which), **needs more**, **planned** (an extension point is specified or extended, and you can follow it) or **declined** (with the reason, for example that it breaks one of the four rules).
- A planned extension point joins the catalogue. The ladder class of your blueprint's rows changes only when it ships. We give no date.
