## Being built

- **[Delivery marketplaces](https://library.ivendnext.app/apps/ivendnext_quickcommerce)**: Orders from delivery marketplaces arrive in iVendNext as sales orders against your store's stock, and your stock levels go back to the marketplace.
- **[M-Pesa payments](https://library.ivendnext.app/apps/ivendnext_pay_mpesa)**: Customers pay with M-Pesa at the till: the payment request goes to their phone.
- **[Paystack payments](https://library.ivendnext.app/apps/ivendnext_pay_paystack)**: Customers pay through Paystack on their own phone at the till, where Paystack works.
- **[Deliveroo](https://library.ivendnext.app/apps/deliveroo)**: Deliveroo orders arrive as sales in your store's stock, and your stock decides what Deliveroo shows as available.
- **[Talabat](https://library.ivendnext.app/apps/talabat)**: Talabat orders arrive as sales in your store's stock, and your stock decides what Talabat shows as available.
- **[Careem](https://library.ivendnext.app/apps/careem)**: Careem orders arrive as sales in your store's stock, and your stock decides what Careem shows as available.
- **[Noon](https://library.ivendnext.app/apps/noon)**: Noon orders arrive as sales in your store's stock.
- **Avalara sales tax**: US and Canadian sales tax worked out by Avalara AvaTax, on the till and in Desk.
- **Amazon**: Amazon orders arrive in iVendNext as sales against your stores' stock, and your stock levels go back to Amazon.
- **NetSuite**: Connects iVendNext to NetSuite: items, prices and customers come from NetSuite, and your stores' sales, returns and payments go back to it.
- **Sage X3**: Connects iVendNext to Sage X3: items, prices and customers come from Sage X3, and your stores' sales, returns and payments go back to it.
