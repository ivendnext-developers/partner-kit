# Videos

Short films for partners and developers on the iVendNext channel: what a partner builds, how to build the app a retail chain needs with the open kit, how to publish it to the App library, and every extension point an app can use. Each film stands on its own, and every film has captions.

**Watch:** the whole series is one playlist, [Build on iVendNext](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUqvJWzjbO1k). For a developer, [the partner developer path](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLYRtn8IR_Fb8) puts the films in the order to watch them.
