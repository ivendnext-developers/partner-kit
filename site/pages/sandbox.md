# Request a sandbox

A sandbox site is your own iVendNext site, hosted by us and full of made-up demo data. It is where you build and test before you touch a customer's site. It is for anyone who builds for an iVendNext customer: an iVendNext Partner, or a licensed customer's IT team.

Your site's address looks like `yourcompany.sandbox.ivend.co`. We tell you the address when the site is ready.

## How to ask

Fill in the sandbox request form on ivendnext.com. Have your GitHub login ready: your sandbox is given to the account you will sign in with on My apps. A licensed customer's IT team may use the same form, or ask its account manager; either way its account owner talks to it. Our team talks to every builder first.

[Open the sandbox request form](https://www.ivendnext.com/developers/sandbox/)

## When access comes

Access means a sandbox site, and the kit's GitHub organisation for the feedback forms. It comes with onboarding, once our team has talked to you. Your sandbox is ready for `ivn try` when it arrives: there is no key to paste.
