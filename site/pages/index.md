## What you can build

A customer's own behaviour, without changing iVendNext itself: the customer's rules at the till, the customer's own records in iVendNext Desk, links to the customer's own systems, reports and approvals, and an AI assistant on the user's own key. [Chapter 1](../../guide/01_welcome.md) says what each means, the [needs catalogue](../../needs/README.md) answers need by need, and [chapter 16](../../guide/16_possible_not_possible.md) gives the limits to know before you quote.

## Build your first app, in six steps

1. **Sign in** with GitHub.
2. **Make the app,** blank or from the sample nearest your customer's need.
3. **Change it.** One file, `manifest.json`, for most apps.
4. **Check it.** The same checker your customer's install runs.
5. **Try it on a site.** Pick a site, or add one. It asks for no key, no plan and no card.
6. **Publish it to your customer.** They install it in one press.

Each step is on [Build your first app](first_app.md), with its commands.
