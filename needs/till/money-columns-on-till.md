## What you build
Nothing yet: no price, amount, tax or tender field is on the field rules' lists, so a rule cannot show or relabel one.

## Limits
- Field rules change what the till shows and asks for, never what it charges.

## Tell the customer
The till cannot yet show or rename a money column; the figures it works out stay as they are.
