## What you build
Nothing. The till has a gift receipt: the cashier picks which lines are gifts, and the receipt prints only those, with no prices and a number for an exchange. It is offered on the finish screen and in Find a sale. The layout (mPOS Gift Receipt) is made when the till is installed. Right after the sale the cashier prints it; for an earlier sale, picking the gift lines needs the reprint right or a manager.

## Limits
None known.

## Tell the customer
The cashier can print a gift receipt for the lines that are gifts, with no prices on it.
