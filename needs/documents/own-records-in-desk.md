## What you build
A manifest declares your record types, fields, print formats, notifications and workspaces, with native screens; they can be listed under ours.

## Limits
- Your workspace is never in the Desk's menu: the retailer's administrator opens it from the App library, and your app's users find it with the search bar.
- A manifest may carry a multi-select field and a settings record of its own (one record, no list) (`guide/07_manifests.md`).

## Tell the customer
Your records live in iVendNext Desk, with their own screens, lists and reports.
